What Drone Photos Add to a Real Estate Listing, and When You Need Them

How aerial photos help waterfront homes, vacant lots and rentals in Sarasota and Southwest Florida, and why your drone pilot should be FAA Part 107 certified.

By Lochlann Fourquin·November 23, 2026·2 min read

Ground photos show what a home looks like. Aerial photos show where it is. In Southwest Florida, where it is is often the selling point.

The questions aerials answer

Buyers and guests have questions that photos from the ground can't answer:

  • How big is the lot? One aerial shows the yard, the setbacks and where the property sits on the street.
  • How close is the water? A drone shot shows the canal, bay or Gulf right there, along with the dock and the route out.
  • What's around it? Golf course, lake, preserve, beach, downtown. Context sells.
  • What's the roof and pool like? Overhead shots show the pool cage, the roof and the outdoor layout.

Homes that benefit most

  • Waterfront and canal homes on Siesta Key, Longboat Key, Bird Key, Venice and Anna Maria Island
  • Vacant land and acreage, where the land is the listing
  • Golf, lake and preserve views in communities like Lakewood Ranch and Palmer Ranch
  • New construction and new communities, to show the layout and amenities
  • Vacation rentals, to prove how close guests are to the beach

Even a standard home in a neighborhood gains from one or two aerials that show the street, the yard and what's nearby.

Why your pilot should be FAA Part 107 certified

Flying a drone for business, real estate marketing included, requires a Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate from the FAA. A certified pilot knows the airspace rules (Sarasota Bradenton International sits right in the middle of the area), how to get authorization near airports and how to fly safely over homes and people. An uncertified pilot can create problems for you as well as for them.

Drone video, too

Aerial footage makes the strongest opening for a listing reel. A slow push toward the home, or a reveal of the water behind it, sets the scene before anyone steps inside.

What about the weather?

Florida weather moves fast. We check conditions the day before. If it isn't safe to fly, the aerials move to the next clear window at no charge.

Ground photos sell the house. Aerials sell the location.

Add aerials to your listing

Every package includes drone photos: 6 with Signature, 10 with Premier and 16 with Platinum. Extra aerials can be added to any shoot. We're FAA Part 107 certified and fly across Sarasota, Venice, Bradenton, Lakewood Ranch, the islands and the rest of Southwest Florida.
